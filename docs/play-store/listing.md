## Full description (max 4000 characters)

dreyev VLink turns your phone into a live dashboard for your car. Plug a VLink (or other ELM327-compatible) OBD-II Bluetooth adapter into the car's diagnostic port, pair it, and see your car's data on clear analog and digital gauges.

WHAT YOU SEE
• Drive: speed, RPM or electric power, pedal position, engine load, brake pressure, steering angle and g-force
• Tires & chassis: pressure and temperature for each tire, wheel speeds, brake pedal
• Vehicle & GPS: VIN, odometer, outside temperature, drive state, and phone GPS
• Map: a full-screen Google map with your drive trace
• Portrait and landscape layouts, dark and light themes

ELECTRIC CARS
The app detects electric cars automatically. On EVs it shows battery power (with regen), state of charge, high-voltage battery data and the READY state. It includes a built-in profile for the Hyundai Ioniq 5 (2022–2024).

KNOW WHERE EACH NUMBER COMES FROM
Every value is labeled with its source: standard OBD-II, manufacturer-specific data, the phone's own sensors, or an estimate. If your car doesn't report something, the app shows N/A instead of guessing.

UNITS YOUR WAY
US units and °F by default, with metric and °C a tap away. Your choice is remembered.

TRIP AND SIGNAL LOGS
Record trips to CSV and use the built-in signal finder to explore your car's data. Logs are saved to Downloads/dreyev-vlink on your phone, and you choose whether to share them.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads and no analytics. Your car's data stays on your phone. The map is loaded from Google Maps.

WHAT YOU NEED
• A VLink or other ELM327-compatible OBD-II adapter (Bluetooth Classic or BLE)
• Android 8.0 or newer
• Try it without a car: Settings › Demo mode runs a simulated vehicle

SAFETY
Mount your phone securely and set it up before you drive. Don't interact with the app while driving. dreyev VLink is a display tool, not a diagnostic or safety system. Data from manufacturer-specific sources can be incomplete or wrong on some models.

dreyev VLink is not affiliated with Vgate, Hyundai, or any car maker.
