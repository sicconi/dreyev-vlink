dreyev VLink Privacy Policy

Effective November 28, 2026

dreyev VLink ("the app") shows live data from your car on your Android phone, using an OBD-II Bluetooth adapter. This policy explains what the app handles and where it goes. In short: the app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your car's data stays on your phone; the only thing that goes online is Google Maps loading the map around you.

Data the app uses on your phone

  • Car data read from the adapter: speed, RPM or motor power, pedal positions, temperatures, tire pressures, battery data, odometer, vehicle identification number (VIN) and similar values. It is shown on screen and kept in memory while the app runs.
  • Phone location from GPS: speed, heading, altitude and the recent drive trace, shown next to the car's data. The app uses location only while you have a dashboard session running (in the app or in its notification). It does not track you in the background.
  • Motion sensors: the phone's accelerometer, used to show g-forces.
  • Settings: your units, theme, chosen adapter and car profile, stored in the app's private storage.
  • Logs you create: when you record a trip or run the signal finder, the app writes a CSV file. Trip logs can include GPS position. These files are saved in the app's storage and copied to Downloads/dreyev-vlink on your phone.

None of this is sent to us. We don't run a server for the app and we never receive your data.

When data leaves your phone

  • When you share it. If you tap Share on a log, Android lets you choose where it goes (email, Drive, Files and so on). That transfer is your choice and is governed by the app you pick.
  • Google Maps. When a map is shown (the Map tab and the GPS card), the app uses the Google Maps SDK for Android, which downloads map tiles from Google. Those requests tell Google roughly which area is on screen. Google's use of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Maps Platform Terms. Your drive trace is drawn on your phone and is not uploaded.
  • Android backup. If you've turned on Android backup, Android may back up the app's settings to your Google account, as it does for other apps. You control this in your phone's settings.

The app has no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and we don't sell or share data with anyone.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth: to find and connect to your OBD-II adapter. Bluetooth scans are not used to work out your location.
  • Location: for GPS speed, heading and the drive trace. On Android 8 to 11, Android also requires it for Bluetooth LE scanning.
  • Notifications: for the notification that shows while the app keeps the adapter connected in the background.
  • Storage (Android 8 and 9 only): to save logs to the Downloads folder.
  • Internet: to load Google Maps tiles. The app has no other network use.

Keeping and deleting data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. Delete individual logs from Downloads/dreyev-vlink with any file manager. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, removes its settings and internal logs. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Children

The app is meant for drivers and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
